Peter Chingaipe reports on the status and performance of the [Asgard/NOTT](https://fys.kuleuven.be/ster/research-projects/nott/nott-asgard/) cold nulling camera, the HAWAII-2RG system that images the photonic chip's nulled outputs at 3.5–4.0 µm. Marc-Antoine Martinod presents the integration status and plans for the full [Asgard](http://asgard-vlti.org/) suite at the VLTI.

I am a co-author of both papers and PI of the ERC-funded SCIFY project that is building NOTT.

Read [Status and performance of the Asgard/NOTT cold nulling camera](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21546) (SPIE 14148, 1414827) and [Pushing high angular resolution and high contrast observations on the VLTI from Y to L band with the Asgard instrumental suite: integration status and plans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24567) (SPIE 14148, 1414808).
