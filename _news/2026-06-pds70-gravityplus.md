PDS 70 is one of only two stars with more than one confirmed protoplanet. A third, closer point-like source had been reported with SPHERE and JWST, but those data could not tell a planet from a compact dust clump.

New [VLTI/GRAVITY+](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/ir/gravityplus) observations of the predicted position did not recover a point source. A fake planet injected at the candidate’s K-band contrast is recovered, so the non-detection is not a sensitivity issue. The long VLTI baselines resolve structure that looks point-like to single-dish imagers; a Jupiter-sized planet would remain unresolved, while a dust clump of order 0.2 au would not. That, together with the candidate’s reflected-starlight spectrum, points to dust rather than a third protoplanet.

Read [Using VLTI/GRAVITY+ to determine the identity of a third planet candidate in the PDS 70 system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26249) in _Astronomy & Astrophysics_ (accepted 13 June 2026).
